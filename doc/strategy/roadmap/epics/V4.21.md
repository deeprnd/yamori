# V4.21: Sector-Relative Positioning

**Labels:** `type/epic`, `type/feature`, `area/investing`
**GitHub Issue:** TBD

A peer set tells you how a company compares to its peers. But is the peer set itself reasonable relative to the sector? V4.21 maps peer-set results to Damodaran sector benchmarks. It compares computed WACC, ROIC, and growth rates against sector medians from Damodaran's published industry data. It flags companies with significant deviation from sector norms and validates whether those deviations are supported by company-specific evidence. The outcome is that every company's valuation is positioned not just relative to its peer set, but relative to its sector — and any deviation from sector norms is flagged and validated against company-specific evidence.

## Product Intent

Deliver the sector-relative positioning engine: map peer-set results to Damodaran sector benchmarks, compare computed WACC, ROIC, and growth rates against sector medians, flag significant deviations from sector norms, and validate deviations against company-specific evidence. The outcome is a sector-relative positioning layer that answers: "Is this company's valuation justified by its fundamentals, or is it an outlier that needs explanation?"

## Users And Jobs

- [Investment analyst]: See whether a company's WACC, ROIC, and growth rate are consistent with its sector — and whether any deviations are supported by company-specific evidence.
- [Compliance reviewer]: Verify that sector benchmarks are from Damodaran's published data, that deviation thresholds are documented, and that flagged deviations are validated against evidence.
- [Engineer]: Add new sector benchmarks (e.g., sector-specific WACC ranges, growth rate percentiles) as policy-configurable options without modifying the core comparison logic.
- [Tickoni tile]: Consume `sectorPositioning(peer_set_results)` as a callable pivot that returns sector-relative positioning with deviation flags.

## Success Metrics

- Sector benchmarks: Damodaran's published industry data for WACC, ROIC, growth rate.
- Deviation flagging: companies with significant deviation from sector norms are flagged.
- Evidence validation: flagged deviations are validated against company-specific evidence.
- Sector consistency: sector assignments from V4.17 used consistently.
- Deterministic: same peer set produces identical sector-relative results across runs.
- Every sector comparison carries the sector benchmark source and vintage.

## Demo Moment

- Expected result: A sector-relative positioning report showing WACC, ROIC, and growth rate compared to sector medians, with deviation flags.

### How Many Stories?

Four stories: benchmark loading (S1), deviation detection (S2), evidence validation (S3), and consistency + provenance (S4). The scope is sector validation — essential for comparative analysis credibility.

### Decomposition Rules

1. **Independent delivery**: Each story produces verifiable sector comparison.
2. **One capability per story**: S1 = benchmarks; S2 = detection; S3 = validation; S4 = consistency.
3. **Split by capability lifecycle**: S1 loads benchmarks; S2 detects deviations; S3 validates; S4 ensures consistency.
4. **Closure story**: S4 returns the complete sector-relative positioning with provenance.

### Bad Splits (Avoid)

- "Deviation detection" + "Evidence validation" as two stories. They are one logical flow — detect then validate.

### Story Line Format

Story IDs follow `VX.Y.SN`. Each story is independently compilable and testable.
