# V1.3: Cost of Capital and WACC

**Labels:** `type/epic`, `type/feature`, `area/investing`
**GitHub Issue:** TBD

Calculates every component of the cost of capital: bottom-up beta from sector unlevered beta and subject-company D/E, revenue-weighted country risk premiums over the 13-block geographic taxonomy, cost of equity via CAPM, cost of debt via synthetic-rating ladder, capital weights, and WACC. Every component is traceable to a Damodaran dataset. This epic closes the Step 2 loop — without WACC, the DCF has no discount rate and cannot produce a present value.

## Product Intent

Deliver the cost-of-capital engine: compute WACC from first principles using bottom-up betas, country risk premiums derived from sovereign default spreads, and synthetic-risk-rated cost of debt. The outcome is a single `WACC` value that is fully traceable to published datasets — no market-implied inputs, no black-box estimates, every component auditable to its source.

## Users And Jobs

- [Investment analyst]: See the WACC breakdown by component (Ke, Kd, equity weight, debt weight) with every dataset source cited.
- [Compliance reviewer]: Verify that CRP is derived from sovereign default spreads × volatility scalar (D22), not read raw from the published table.
- [Engineer]: Extend the cost-of-capital pipeline by adding a new synthetic-rating tier or an alternative CRP basis (lambda) as a policy switch.
- [Tickoni tile]: Consume `computeWACC(inputs, datasets)` as a callable capability that returns a fully auditable WACC with component breakdown.

## Success Metrics

- Bottom-up beta: sector unlevered beta relevered at subject-company D/E.
- CRP: sovereign default spread × relative equity volatility scalar (D22); `DefaultSpread` and `Crp` are distinct comptime types (§3.1).
- Cost of equity: `Rf + beta × ERP + weightedCRP` via CAPM.
- Cost of debt: risk-free rate + default spread (synthetic-rating ladder) × (1 − tax rate).
- Capital weights: market-value equity and debt weights.
- WACC: `Ke × We + Kd × Wd × (1 − tax)`.
- Every CRP block uses median of member-country CRPs (D22b), no GDP or market-cap weighting.
- Geographic exposure uses revenue-weighted 13-block taxonomy (D22c).
- Sanity benchmark: computed WACC within a band of the sector benchmark (D8, audit check `WACC_VS_INDUSTRY`).

## Demo Moment

- Command or flow: `tickoni valuation run STLA --show-wacc`
- Expected result: A WACC breakdown showing Ke, Kd, equity weight, debt weight, beta, CRP per block, and the final WACC — all traceable to dataset vintages.

## Scope

### In Scope

- Bottom-up beta: relevering sector unlevered beta at subject-company D/E (us_betas, global_beta datasets).
- CRP computation: sovereign default spread → CRP via volatility scalar (§3.1); `DefaultSpread` vs `Crp` comptime types prevent accidental misuse.
- Revenue-weighted CRP: Σ (exposure weight × CRP) over 13-block geographic taxonomy (D22).
- Cost of equity: CAPM formula `Rf + beta × ERP + CRP` (us_implied_erp dataset).
- Synthetic-rating ladder: cost of debt via interest coverage ratio → synthetic rating → default spread (rating_risk_premiums dataset).
- Cost of debt: `Rf + default_spread × (1 − tax rate)`.
- Capital weights: market-value equity and debt.
- WACC: weighted Ke and after-tax Kd.
- Geographic exposure: required `GeoExposure` input with 13-block taxonomy; composite regions mapped from filing disclosures (§4.3).
- Sanity benchmark: WACC vs industry benchmark from us_wacc / global_wacc datasets (never an input, only a cross-check).
- Provenance: every WACC component carries its dataset source, column, and vintage.

### Out Of Scope

- R&D capitalization adjustment (V2.7).
- Lease capitalization adjustment (V2.8).
- Three-stage growth model (V1.4).
- FCFF projection (V1.4).
- Terminal value (V1.5).
- Equity bridge (V1.5).

### How Many Stories?

Eight stories: beta (S1), CRP comptime types (S2), weighted CRP (S3), cost of equity (S4), synthetic rating (S5), cost of debt (S6), capital weights + WACC (S7), provenance + benchmark (S8). Each delivers an independently testable computation. The scope is narrow (cost of capital only), so 8 stories reflects the number of distinct financial computations.

### Decomposition Rules

1. **Independent delivery**: Each story compiles and produces a verifiable computation.
2. **One capability per story**: S1 = beta; S2 = CRP types; S3 = weighted CRP; S4 = Ke; S5 = synthetic rating; S6 = Kd; S7 = WACC; S8 = provenance + benchmark.
3. **Split by capability lifecycle**: S1–S3 compute risk inputs; S4–S6 compute cost components; S7 combines them; S8 adds provenance.
4. **Closure story**: S8 ensures all WACC components carry provenance and the industry benchmark cross-check.

### Bad Splits (Avoid)

- "Cost of equity" + "Cost of debt" as one story. They use different datasets and formulas; independently testable.
- "Capital weights" + "WACC" as two stories. They are computed together: weights are needed for WACC.

### Story Line Format

Story IDs follow `VX.Y.SN`. Each story is independently compilable and testable.
