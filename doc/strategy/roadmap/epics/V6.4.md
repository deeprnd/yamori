Volatility and trend indicators tell the analyst where price is heading and how violently it's moving. Bollinger Bands define dynamic support/resistance envelopes around a moving average; ATR measures average price range regardless of direction; ADX quantifies trend strength; Parabolic SAR provides trailing stop levels. V6.4 implements Bollinger Bands (upper/middle/lower), ATR, ADX, and Parabolic SAR from TA-Lib. These are the first V6 indicators that produce envelopes and trend-strength measures rather than simple signal series. Bollinger Bands compose over a moving average from V6.2, requiring standard deviation computation over the lookback period.

## Product Intent

Implement volatility envelopes (Bollinger Bands), trend strength (ADX, ATR), and trailing stop (Parabolic SAR) from TA-Lib as formula nodes on the dependency graph. These indicators produce volatility envelopes and trend-strength measures that complement the oscillator signals from V6.3.

## Users And Jobs

- [Investment analyst]: Compute Bollinger Bands to identify overbought/oversold relative to recent volatility, ATR to measure price range, and ADX to gauge trend strength.
- [Engineer]: Validate that Bollinger Bands correctly compute upper/middle/lower bands from SMA + standard deviation, and that ADX correctly derives from +DI/-DI.
- [Compliance reviewer]: Verify that all indicator dependencies resolve correctly in topological order and produce deterministic results.

## Success Metrics

- Bollinger Bands (upper/middle/lower), ATR, ADX, Parabolic SAR all implemented from TA-Lib.
