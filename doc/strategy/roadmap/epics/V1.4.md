# V1.4: Three-Stage Growth Model and FCFF Projection

**Labels:** `type/epic`, `type/feature`, `area/investing`
**GitHub Issue:** TBD

Derives the complete three-stage growth schedule: high-growth rate from sustainable ROIC × sustainable reinvestment, continuous linear transition paths for growth, ROIC, and tax rate via GSL interpolation, terminal growth constrained to risk-free rate, and ten-year EBIT → NOPAT → reinvestment → FCFF projection. This epic closes the Step 3–4 loop — without the growth model, WACC has no denominator and the DCF has no numerator.

## Product Intent

Deliver the growth projection engine: transform sustainable ROIC and reinvestment rate into a ten-year forecast of EBIT, NOPAT, reinvestment, and FCFF with continuous transition paths and constrained terminal growth. The outcome is a complete forward-looking operating model that is fully deterministic and traceable to the historical evidence base from V1.2.

## Users And Jobs

- [Investment analyst]: See the ten-year FCFF projection with high-growth, transition, and stable-growth stages clearly delineated.
- [Compliance reviewer]: Verify that transition paths are continuous linear interpolations (GSL), not step functions; that terminal growth ≤ risk-free rate; and that `g(t) = ROIC(t) × reinvestmentRate(t)` holds every year.
- [Engineer]: Extend the projection pipeline by adding alternative transition methods (exponential decay, hockey-stick) as a policy switch.
- [Tickoni tile]: Consume `projectFCFF(policy, growthInputs)` as a callable capability that returns a ten-year FCFF schedule.

## Success Metrics

- High-growth rate: `sustainableROIC × sustainableReinvestmentRate` with explicit cap.
- Transition paths: linear interpolation via GSL for growth, ROIC, and tax rate (Years 6–10).
- Terminal growth constrained: `g_stable ≤ risk_free_rate`.
- Ten-year EBIT projection: `EBIT(t) = EBIT(t-1) × (1 + growth(t))`.
- Ten-year NOPAT projection: `NOPAT(t) = EBIT(t) × (1 - taxRate(t))`.
- Ten-year reinvestment projection: `Reinvestment(t) = NOPAT(t) × reinvestmentRate(t)`.
- Ten-year FCFF projection: `FCFF(t) = NOPAT(t) - Reinvestment(t)`.
- Tax convergence: effective rate years 1–5 → marginal rate by year 10 (D10).
- Audit check: `g(t) = ROIC(t) × reinvestmentRate(t)` holds every year (Step 10).
- Audit check: ROIC path continuous at stage-2/terminal boundary (Step 10).

## Demo Moment

- Command or flow: `tickoni valuation run STLA --show-projection`
- Expected result: A ten-year table of EBIT, NOPAT, reinvestment, and FCFF with stage boundaries clearly labeled.

## Scope

### In Scope

- High-growth rate: `sustainableROIC × sustainableReinvestmentRate` with methodology's explicit cap.
- GSL interpolation: linear transition paths for growth, ROIC, and tax rate (Years 6–10).
- Terminal growth constraint: `g_stable ≤ risk_free_rate` (audit check `TERMINAL_G_LE_RF`).
- EBIT projection path: compound annual growth from Year 1 to Year 10.
- NOPAT projection path: `EBIT(t) × (1 - taxRate(t))` per year.
- Reinvestment projection path: `NOPAT(t) × reinvestmentRate(t)` per year.
- FCFF projection path: `NOPAT(t) - Reinvestment(t)` per year.
- Tax rate convergence: effective rate years 1–5 → marginal rate by year 10 (D10).
- Derived reinvestment path: `growth(t) / ROIC(t)` for cross-check.
- Stable growth: terminal growth selected and constrained per risk-free rate and macro assumptions.
- Audit checks: `g(t) = ROIC(t) × reinvestmentRate(t)` every year; ROIC path continuity at stage boundary.
- Provenance: every projected metric carries its source assumption, formula, and input citations.

### How Many Stories?

Nine stories: high-growth rate (S1), interpolation (S2), terminal constraint (S3), EBIT projection (S4), NOPAT projection (S5), reinvestment projection (S6), FCFF projection (S7), tax convergence (S8), and audit + provenance (S9). Each delivers an independently testable computation. The scope is moderate (growth model + projection), so 9 stories reflects the number of distinct projected series.

### Decomposition Rules

1. **Independent delivery**: Each story compiles and produces a verifiable computation.
2. **One capability per story**: S1 = high-growth rate; S2 = interpolation; S3 = terminal constraint; S4 = EBIT path; S5 = NOPAT path; S6 = reinvestment path; S7 = FCFF path; S8 = tax convergence; S9 = audit + provenance.
3. **Split by capability lifecycle**: S1–S3 establish the growth parameters; S4–S7 project the series; S8 handles tax convergence; S9 adds audit checks.
4. **Closure story**: S9 ensures all projections pass audit checks and carry provenance.

### Bad Splits (Avoid)

- "EBIT projection" + "NOPAT projection" as two stories. They are computed sequentially from the same growth path; splitting adds no independent testability.
- "FCFF" + "Reinvestment" as two stories. FCFF is defined as NOPAT − Reinvestment; they are one computation.

### Story Line Format

Story IDs follow `VX.Y.SN`. Each story is independently compilable and testable.
