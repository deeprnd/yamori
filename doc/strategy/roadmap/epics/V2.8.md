
# V2.3: Cost of Capital and WACC

**Labels:** `type/epic`, `type/enhancement`, `area/finance`
**GitHub Issue:** #9

Let users view a complete WACC breakdown showing beta, country risk premiums, cost of equity, cost of debt, capital weights, and final WACC — each component traceable to its source dataset and methodology. Users can inspect every step of the calculation to understand how company-specific risk is translated into a single discount rate.

## Product Intent

Deliver the cost of capital computation: bottom-up beta, country risk premiums, cost of equity (CAPM), cost of debt (synthetic rating), capital weights, and WACC — all traceable to Damodaran datasets and registered as formula nodes on the dependency graph. The outcome is a fully auditable, component-by-component WACC that users can inspect, trace, and validate.

## Users And Jobs

- [Investment analyst]: View a complete WACC breakdown showing beta, CRP, cost of equity, cost of debt, capital weights, and final WACC — each component traceable to source data.
- [Investment analyst]: Compare the WACC components against sector benchmarks to assess whether the inputs are reasonable.
- [Data researcher]: Add new WACC formula nodes to the dependency graph without modifying existing components.
- [Portfolio manager]: Use WACC as the discount rate in scenario analysis and sensitivity testing.
- [Compliance reviewer]: Verify that each WACC component uses the correct Damodaran dataset and methodology, and that the synthetic rating ladder is applied correctly.

## Success Metrics

- Bottom-up beta: sector unlevered beta × (1 + (1 − tax) × D/E) for the subject company.
- Country risk premiums: revenue-weighted over the 13-block geographic taxonomy.
- Cost of equity via CAPM: risk-free rate + beta × equity risk premium + CRP.
- Cost of debt via synthetic-rating ladder: subject-company D/E maps to a synthetic rating, which maps to a yield spread.
- Capital weights: D/(D+E) and E/(D+E) from the subject-company capital structure.
- WACC: cost of equity × E/(D+E) + cost of debt × (1 − tax) × D/(D+E).
- Every component traceable to a Damodaran dataset.
- All computations expressed as formula nodes on the dependency graph.

### How Many Stories?

Six stories: beta (S1), CRP (S2), cost of equity (S3), cost of debt (S4), capital weights (S5), and WACC (S6). The scope is the complete cost of capital framework — every component is a distinct, independently verifiable calculation.

### Decomposition Rules

1. **Independent delivery**: Each story compiles and produces a verifiable WACC component.
2. **One capability per story**: S1 = beta; S2 = CRP; S3 = cost of equity; S4 = cost of debt; S5 = weights; S6 = WACC.
3. **Split by capability lifecycle**: S1–S2 establish the risk inputs; S3–S4 establish the cost components; S5–S6 establish the aggregation.
4. **Closure story**: S6 returns the final WACC — the evidence layer for the valuation runner.

### Bad Splits (Avoid)

- "Beta" + "CRP" as separate epics. They are part of the same risk assessment framework.
- "Cost of equity" + "Cost of debt" as two stories. They are the same cost-of-capital analysis with different inputs.

### Story Line Format

Story IDs follow `VX.Y.SN`. Each story is independently compilable and testable.
