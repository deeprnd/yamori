# V9.4: Fixed Income Instruments

**Labels:** `type/epic`, `type/enhancement`, `area/finance`, `area/build`
**GitHub Issue:** TBD

<!-- One paragraph: what complete feature this epic delivers and why now.
     This is market-facing — (lead with WHY,

     process; describe the transformation. Stories stay technical.-->

V9.4 extends Yamori beyond equity derivatives into fixed income — bonds, interest rate swaps, caps, floors, and swaptions — the asset classes that drive institutional portfolio construction and monetary policy. By implementing bond pricing (zero-coupon, coupon-bearing) with yield curve interpolation (linear, cubic, Nelson-Siegel) and day-count convention handling, V9.4 provides the yield curve infrastructure that feeds back into the Damodaran cost-of-debt and risk-free rate lookups (M2). Swap pricing requires bootstrapping from market quotes; cap/floor pricing requires volatility surface construction; swaptions require tree-based engines. These instruments also produce the yield curve objects that V9.5 stores and manages as shared-memory constructs.

## Product Intent

Deliver fixed income instrument pricing: zero-coupon and coupon-bearing bonds with yield curve interpolation (linear, cubic, Nelson-Siegel) and day-count convention handling; interest rate swaps via bootstrapping from market quotes; caps and floors via volatility surface construction; swaptions via tree-based engines. Bond pricing requires yield curve interpolation and day-count convention handling — the infrastructure that feeds back into Damodaran cost-of-debt and risk-free rate lookups (M2). Swap pricing requires bootstrapping from market quotes. Cap/floor pricing requires volatility surface construction. These instruments also provide the yield curve objects that V9.5 stores as shared-memory constructs. Deterministic execution policy pins the interpolation method, day-count convention, and bootstrapping algorithm.

## Users And Jobs

- [Fixed income analyst]: Price zero-coupon and coupon-bearing bonds through Yamori's dependency graph — receiving fair value, yield to maturity, and duration/convexity with pinned interpolation method and day-count convention.
- [Interest rate strategist]: Price interest rate swaps, caps, and floors through Yamori's dependency graph — receiving fair value and risk measures with bootstrapped yield curves and constructed volatility surfaces.
- [Risk manager]: Compute bond duration and convexity, swap PV01, and cap/floor sensitivities — deterministic, auditable, reproducible with pinned interpolation method and bootstrapping algorithm.
- [Tickoni tile]: Consume `priceBond(curve, coupon, maturity, dayCount)` and `priceSwap(curve, fixedRate, tenor)` as callable pivots that return fair value and risk measures through shared memory.
