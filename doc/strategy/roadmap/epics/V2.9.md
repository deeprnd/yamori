
# V2.4: Three-Stage Growth Model and FCFF Projection

**Labels:** `type/epic`, `type/enhancement`, `area/finance`
**GitHub Issue:** #10

Let users project a ten-year FCFF schedule derived from the company's sustainable ROIC and reinvestment rate, with transparent transition paths from high-growth to stable-growth. The model uses deterministic interpolation — no tolerance-based solvers — so every projected value is traceable to its inputs and reproducible.

## Product Intent

Deliver the three-stage growth model: derive sustainable growth rate from ROIC × reinvestment, generate continuous linear transition paths for growth, ROIC, and tax rate using GSL linear interpolation, constrain terminal growth to the risk-free rate, and project a ten-year EBIT → NOPAT → reinvestment → FCFF schedule. The outcome is a complete, deterministic FCFF projection that users can view and trace back to their inputs.

## Users And Jobs

- [Investment analyst]: View a ten-year FCFF projection derived from the company's sustainable ROIC and reinvestment rate, with transparent transition paths from high-growth to stable-growth.
- [Investment analyst]: Compare projected growth rates against historical performance to assess whether the model's assumptions are reasonable.
- [Data researcher]: Add new growth model formula nodes to the dependency graph without modifying existing transition path logic.
- [Portfolio manager]: Use the FCFF projection as the basis for scenario analysis and sensitivity testing.
- [Compliance reviewer]: Verify that the growth model uses sustainable inputs derived from historical data, that transition paths are continuous and deterministic, and that terminal growth does not exceed the risk-free rate.

## Success Metrics

- High-growth rate: sustainable ROIC × sustainable reinvestment rate.
- Transition paths: continuous linear interpolation for growth, ROIC, and tax rate (GSL linear interpolation) over a configurable transition period.
- Terminal growth constraint: terminal growth rate does not exceed the risk-free rate.
- Ten-year FCFF projection: EBIT → NOPAT → reinvestment → FCFF for each of ten years.
- Deterministic interpolation: no tolerance-based solvers, fixed interpolation steps.
- All computations expressed as formula nodes on the dependency graph.

### How Many Stories?

Five stories: high-growth rate (S1), transition paths (S2), terminal constraint (S3), ten-year projection (S4), and deterministic interpolation (S5). The scope is the complete growth model — from sustainable inputs to FCFF projection.

### Decomposition Rules

1. **Independent delivery**: Each story compiles and produces a verifiable growth model component.
2. **One capability per story**: S1 = high-growth rate; S2 = transition paths; S3 = terminal constraint; S4 = projection; S5 = interpolation determinism.
3. **Split by capability lifecycle**: S1 establishes the growth inputs; S2–S3 establish the transition logic; S4–S5 establish the projection and determinism.
4. **Closure story**: S5 returns the complete ten-year FCFF schedule — the evidence layer for the terminal value computation.

### Bad Splits (Avoid)

- "High-growth rate" + "Transition paths" as separate epics. They are part of the same growth framework.
- "Ten-year projection" + "Deterministic interpolation" as two stories. The projection needs deterministic interpolation.

### Story Line Format

Story IDs follow `VX.Y.SN`. Each story is independently compilable and testable.
