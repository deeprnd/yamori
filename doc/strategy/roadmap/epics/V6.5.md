# V6.5: Volume Indicators and Pattern Recognition

**Labels:** `type/epic`, `type/enhancement`, `area/finance`
**GitHub Issue:** TBD

<!-- One paragraph: what complete feature this epic delivers and why now.
     This is market-facing — (lead with WHY,
     reframe constraints as trust signals). Do NOT describe the tool or
     process; describe the transformation. Stories stay technical.-->

Volume confirms price. On-Balance Volume tracks cumulative buying and selling pressure; Accumulation/Distribution measures money flow weighted by price position within the day's range; AD Oscillator signals shifts in accumulation/distribution momentum. Candlestick pattern recognition identifies chart formations — doji, hammer, engulfing — that experienced traders read intuitively but machines process deterministically. V6.5 implements OBV, AD, ADOSC, and TA-Lib's candlestick pattern recognition API. Volume indicators produce cumulative or smoothed series from price + volume data; pattern recognition produces boolean or categorical signals from candlestick formations. Both are formula nodes on the dependency graph.

## Product Intent

Implement volume indicators (OBV, AD, ADOSC) and candlestick pattern recognition from TA-Lib as formula nodes on the dependency graph. Volume indicators produce cumulative or smoothed series from price + volume data, complementing the price-only indicators from V6.2–V6.4. Pattern recognition produces boolean or categorical signals from candlestick formations — the first V6 capability that translates discrete chart patterns into machine-readable signals.

## Users And Jobs

- [Investment analyst]: Compute volume indicators to confirm price trends and identify accumulation/distribution phases; use candlestick patterns for pattern-based signals.
- [Engineer]: Validate that volume indicators correctly integrate price + volume data, and that pattern recognition returns boolean/categorical signals for each candlestick type.
- [Compliance reviewer]: Verify that pattern recognition signals are deterministic and reproducible across platforms.
