

# V3.6: Financial Statement Ingestion and XBRL Mapping

**Labels:** `type/epic`, `type/enhancement`, `area/finance`
**GitHub Issue:** TBD

Parse SEC 10-K and 10-Q XBRL filings, map US-GAAP and IFRS elements to canonical metric names, normalize period alignment, and produce typed Yamori series with full provenance. Every metric traces back to its source XBRL element, filing document, period, and currency. This closes the gap between raw regulatory data and the structured inputs the valuation engine requires — without it, the engine can only consume hand-crafted JSON inputs. The outcome is that any SEC filing can be ingested and transformed into clean, typed financial series ready for valuation.

## Product Intent

Let users drop a company's 10-K or 10-Q filing and receive a clean financial frame with all standard metrics (Revenue, EBIT, Net Income, Total Assets, etc.) ready for valuation. Parse XBRL filings from SEC EDGAR, map US-GAAP and IFRS elements to canonical Yamori metric names, normalize period alignment, and produce typed financial series with full provenance. The outcome is that any SEC filing can be ingested and transformed into a clean financial frame that M1–M4 consume.

## Users And Jobs

- [Investment analyst]: Drop a company's 10-K or 10-Q filing and receive a clean financial frame with all standard metrics (Revenue, EBIT, Net Income, Total Assets, etc.) ready for valuation.

- [Data researcher]: Extend the XBRL mapping by adding new elements from new filing types (13-F, 8-K) without modifying the core ingestion logic.
- [Portfolio manager]: Batch-ingest multiple company filings and compare their financial frames side by side.

### How Many Stories?

Six stories: parsing foundation (S1), two taxonomy mappings (S2, S3), period/currency normalization (S4), provenance (S5), and structure handling + error rejection (S6). The scope is data ingestion — narrow but foundational.

### Decomposition Rules

1. **Independent delivery**: Each story compiles and produces verifiable parsed output.
2. **One capability per story**: S1 = parsing; S2 = US-GAAP map; S3 = IFRS map; S4 = normalization; S5 = provenance; S6 = structure + errors.
3. **Split by capability lifecycle**: S1–S3 handle taxonomy parsing; S4 normalizes; S5 adds provenance; S6 handles structure and errors.
4. **Closure story**: S6 ensures all filings are parsed with proper structure validation and error handling.

### Bad Splits (Avoid)

- "US-GAAP" + "IFRS" as one story. They use different taxonomies and may need different mappings.
- "Period alignment" + "Currency normalization" as two stories. They are applied together during normalization.

### Story Line Format

Story IDs follow `VX.Y.SN`. Each story is independently compilable and testable.
