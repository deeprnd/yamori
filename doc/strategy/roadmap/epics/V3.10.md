

# V3.10: Base-Year Normalization and Distortion Detection

**Labels:** `type/epic`, `type/enhancement`, `area/finance`
**GitHub Issue:** TBD

See whether the most recent margin is distorted and whether EBIT has been normalized, with the historical median and distortion percentage visible. Detect when the current margin deviates significantly (>30%) from the five-year historical median, normalize EBIT using the historical median margin, and document every normalization decision in the provenance chain. A company's most recent fiscal year may have an abnormal margin due to a one-time event, cyclical downturn, or accounting change. Using that distorted year as the base for projections embeds the distortion into every future year's forecast. This adjustment corrects that by anchoring valuations to sustainable margins rather than the noise of the most recent period.

## Product Intent

Let users see whether the most recent margin is distorted and whether EBIT has been normalized, with the historical median and distortion percentage visible. Detect when the current margin deviates significantly (>30%) from the five-year historical median, normalize EBIT using the median margin, and document every normalization decision in the provenance chain. The outcome is that stage-1 growth assumptions are grounded in historical evidence, not the most recent period's noise — the same principle that outlier filtering applies to ROIC but extended to margins.

## Users And Jobs

- [Investment analyst]: See whether the most recent margin is distorted and whether EBIT has been normalized, with the historical median and distortion percentage visible.
- [Compliance reviewer]: Verify that the 30% distortion threshold is applied consistently and that normalized EBIT is derived from historical median margin, not from an asserted target.
- [Data researcher]: Adjust the distortion threshold (e.g., 20% for cyclicals) as a policy switch.
- [Portfolio manager]: Compare normalized and unnormalized base-year EBIT to understand how normalization affects valuation.

## Success Metrics

- Base-year distortion detection: current margin deviating >30% from five-year median.
- Normalized EBIT: current revenue × five-year median margin when distortion detected.
- Validation: stage-1 inputs derived from history, not assertion.
- Normalization decision reported in provenance (detected vs not detected, margin used).
- Consistent application: normalization applied before all downstream computations (ROIC, growth, FCFF).
- Every normalized metric carries the normalization provenance.

### How Many Stories?

Four stories: median calculation (S1), distortion detection (S2), normalized EBIT (S3), and validation + provenance (S4). The scope is narrow (one normalization logic), so 4 stories is sufficient.

### Decomposition Rules

1. **Independent delivery**: Each story produces verifiable normalized financial data.
2. **One capability per story**: S1 = median; S2 = detection; S3 = normalization; S4 = validation.
3. **Split by capability lifecycle**: S1 computes the median; S2 applies the threshold; S3 normalizes; S4 validates.
4. **Closure story**: S4 ensures the normalization decision is validated and documented.

### Bad Splits (Avoid)

- "Distortion detection" + "Normalized EBIT" as two stories. Detection triggers normalization — they are one logical flow.

### Story Line Format

Story IDs follow `VX.Y.SN`. Each story is independently compilable and testable.
