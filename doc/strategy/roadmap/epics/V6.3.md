# V6.3: Oscillator Indicators

**Labels:** `type/epic`, `type/feature`, `area/investing`
**GitHub Issue:** TBD

<!-- One paragraph: what complete feature this epic delivers and why now.
     This is market-facing — (lead with WHY,
     reframe constraints as trust signals). Do NOT describe the tool or
     process; describe the transformation. Stories stay technical.-->

Oscillators are the workhorses of technical analysis: RSI measures momentum exhaustion, Stochastic identifies overbought/oversold conditions, CCI flags extreme price deviations, ROC and MOM quantify rate of change. V6.3 implements RSI, Stochastic (Fast/D Slow), CCI, ROC, and Momentum from TA-Lib as formula nodes on the dependency graph. Each oscillator is a bounded or unbounded signal series derived from the price data. RSI and Stochastic depend on moving averages from V6.2, making this the first epic whose indicators compose over other indicator outputs.

## Product Intent

Implement the core oscillator family from TA-Lib as formula nodes on the dependency graph. RSI, Stochastic, CCI, ROC, and Momentum each produce signal series that analysts use to identify momentum, overbought/oversold conditions, and trend reversals. Oscillators compose over moving averages, making this the first cross-indicator dependency.

## Users And Jobs

- [Investment analyst]: Compute RSI, Stochastic, CCI, ROC, and Momentum on the same price series to identify momentum shifts and overbought/oversold zones.
- [Engineer]: Validate that oscillator indicators producing bounded outputs (RSI 0–100, Stochastic 0–100) are correct, and that unbounded outputs (CCI, ROC, MOM) are correct.
- [Compliance reviewer]: Verify that oscillator dependencies on moving averages (V6.2) resolve correctly in topological order.

## Success Metrics

- RSI, Stochastic (Fast/D Slow), CCI, ROC, Momentum all implemented from TA-Lib.
- Each oscillator is a formula node on the dependency graph.
- RSI produces bounded output (0–100); Stochastic produces bounded output (0–100).
- CCI, ROC, MOM produce unbounded output series.
- Oscillator dependencies on moving averages (V6.2) resolve correctly.
- Bit-identical outputs across all four target platforms.

## Demo Moment

- Command or flow: `tickoni indicator compute --type=RSI --period=14 --input=price_series`
- Expected result: 14-period RSI computed deterministically, bounded to 0–100, bit-identical across platforms.

## Scope

### In Scope

-  RSI, Stochastic (Fast/D Slow), CCI, ROC, Momentum from TA-Lib.
-  Formula nodes on the dependency graph.
-  Oscillator dependencies on moving averages (V6.2): RSI → SMA/EMA, Stochastic → SMA, CCI → SMA + mean deviation.
-  Deterministic execution policy.
-  Platform-identical output validation.

### Out Of Scope

-  Volatility indicators (Bollinger Bands, ATR) — V6.4.
-  Volume indicators (OBV, AD) — V6.5.
-  Candlestick pattern recognition — V6.5.
-  Demo workflow — V6.6.
