
# V7.6: Portfolio Simulation and Scenario Analysis

**Labels:** `type/epic`, `type/enhancement`, `area/finance`
**GitHub Issue:** TBD

<!-- One paragraph: what complete feature this epic delivers and why now.-->

V7.5 produced optimized portfolios; V7.6 tests them under stress. Portfolio simulation subjects optimized weights to historical and Monte Carlo scenarios, producing distributions of portfolio values, drawdown analysis, and tail risk metrics. BLAS-accelerated matrix operations make large-scale simulation tractable. This epic shares the RNG infrastructure and deterministic execution policy from M4 and the dependency graph from V7.5 — optimized weights are inputs to simulation nodes.

## Product Intent

Simulate portfolio returns under historical scenarios (V7.7) and Monte Carlo scenarios (using M4's RNG infrastructure) with BLAS-accelerated matrix operations. Produce distribution of portfolio values, drawdown analysis, and tail risk metrics. Shares RNG infrastructure and deterministic execution policy from M1/M4. Portfolio simulation uses the same dependency graph as V7.5 — optimized weights are inputs to simulation nodes.

## Users And Jobs

- [Portfolio manager]: Test optimized portfolios under historical scenarios and Monte Carlo simulations to understand potential outcomes.
- [Risk manager]: Compute drawdown distributions and tail risk metrics for stress testing.
- [Compliance reviewer]: Verify that simulation results are deterministic and reproducible.
