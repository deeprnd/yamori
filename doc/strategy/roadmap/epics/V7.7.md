# V7.7: Historical Scenario Analysis and Backtesting

**Labels:** `type/epic`, `type/feature`, `area/investing`
**GitHub Issue:** TBD

<!-- One paragraph: what complete feature this epic delivers and why now.-->

V7.6 tested portfolios under single scenarios; V7.7 tests them across time. Historical scenario analysis runs portfolio optimization and risk decomposition across multiple historical periods, producing time-series of optimal weights, rolling risk metrics, and out-of-sample performance. Backtesting validates optimization stability: weight drift, turnover, and rebalance frequency. This is the final M7 epic — it closes the milestone by proving that portfolio analytics works end-to-end through Yamori's dependency graph, deterministic execution policy, and shared-memory infrastructure.

## Product Intent

Run portfolio optimization and risk decomposition across historical periods, producing time-series of optimal weights, rolling risk metrics, and out-of-sample performance. Backtest optimization stability: weight drift, turnover, rebalance frequency. Share historical data infrastructure from M5 (historical replay). Deterministic execution policy pins the lookback window and rebalance frequency.

## Users And Jobs

- [Portfolio manager]: Validate that optimized portfolios are stable across historical periods — not just overfit to one regime.
- [Quantitative analyst]: Track weight drift and turnover to assess rebalancing costs.
- [Researcher]: Compare in-sample vs. out-of-sample performance across different optimization objectives.
