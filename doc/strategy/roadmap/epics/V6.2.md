# V6.2: Moving Average Indicators

**Labels:** `type/epic`, `type/enhancement`, `area/finance`
**GitHub Issue:** TBD

<!-- One paragraph: what complete feature this epic delivers and why now.
     This is market-facing — (lead with WHY,
     reframe constraints as trust signals). Do NOT describe the tool or
     process; describe the transformation. Stories stay technical.-->

Moving averages are the foundational building blocks of technical analysis: every oscillator, volatility indicator, and trend measure derives from a smoothed price series. V6.2 implements simple, exponential, weighted, double/triple exponential, Kaufman adaptive, MAMA, and T3 moving averages from TA-Lib. Each is a formula node that accepts a price series and period parameter and produces a filtered series. These are not standalone tools — they are the substrate for Bollinger Bands, MACD, and all oscillators that follow. The outcome is the first complete family of technical indicators registered on Yamori's dependency graph.

## Product Intent

Implement all major moving average types from TA-Lib as formula nodes on the dependency graph. Each moving average accepts a price series and period parameter, producing a deterministic filtered series. Moving averages serve as building blocks for higher-level indicators in subsequent epics.

## Users And Jobs

- [Investment analyst]: Apply multiple moving average types to the same price series and compare their smoothing characteristics.
- [Engineer]: Validate that each moving average type produces deterministic, bit-identical outputs across all target platforms.
- [Compliance reviewer]: Verify that period parameters are validated and that the TA-Lib implementation is pinned.

## Success Metrics

- SMA, EMA, WMA, DEMA, TEMA, KAMA, MAMA, T3 moving averages all implemented from TA-Lib.
- Each moving average is a formula node accepting price series + period.
- Deterministic execution policy pins the TA-Lib implementation.
- Bit-identical outputs across Linux x86, Linux ARM, macOS ARM, and Windows x86.
- Moving averages produce correct filtered series for standard test inputs.

## Demo Moment

- Command or flow: `tickoni indicator compute --type=SMA --period=20 --input=price_series`
- Expected result: 20-period simple moving average computed deterministically over the price series, bit-identical across platforms.
