# V5.29: Sector-Relative Positioning

**Labels:** `type/epic`, `type/feature`, `area/investing`, `area/analysis`
**GitHub Issue:** TBD

Maps peer-set results to Damodaran sector benchmarks. Compares computed WACC, ROIC, and growth rates against sector medians from Damodaran's published industry data. Flags companies with significant deviation from sector norms and validates whether those deviations are supported by company-specific evidence. The outcome is that every company's valuation is contextualized within its sector — not just relative to peers, but relative to the broader industry benchmarks that institutional analysts use.

## Product Intent

Deliver the sector-relative positioning engine: map peer-set results to Damodaran sector benchmarks, comparing computed WACC, ROIC, and growth rates against sector medians from Damodaran's published industry data. Flag companies with significant deviation from sector norms and validate whether those deviations are supported by company-specific evidence. The outcome is sector-contextualized valuation — every company is evaluated not just against peers, but against the industry benchmarks that institutional analysts rely on.

## Users And Jobs

- [Investment analyst]: See how each company's WACC, ROIC, and growth rate compare to sector medians — and whether deviations from the sector norm are justified by company-specific evidence.
- [Compliance reviewer]: Verify that sector benchmarks come from Damodaran's published industry data, that deviations are flagged consistently, and that validation is evidence-based.
- [Engineer]: Extend the sector benchmark database by adding new Damodaran sectors without modifying the comparison logic.
- [Tickoni tile]: Consume `compareSector(company, valuations)` as a callable pivot that returns sector-relative positioning with deviation flags.

### How Many Stories?

Four stories: sector benchmark database (S1), deviation flagging (S2), evidence-based validation (S3), and deterministic execution (S4). Sector positioning is the final layer of comparative analysis — contextualizing peer-set results within industry benchmarks.

### Decomposition Rules

1. **Independent delivery**: Each story produces verifiable sector positioning.
2. **One capability per story**: S1 = benchmarks; S2 = deviation flags; S3 = evidence validation; S4 = determinism.
3. **Split by capability lifecycle**: S1 loads sector benchmarks; S2 flags deviations; S3 validates evidence; S4 guarantees determinism.
4. **Closure story**: S4 returns deterministic sector positioning with evidence-based validation.

### Bad Splits (Avoid)

- "Deviation flagging" + "Evidence-based validation" as two stories. They are the same analytical capability — flagging a deviation is incomplete without validating it.

### Story Line Format

Story IDs follow `VX.Y.SN`. Each story is independently compilable and testable.
