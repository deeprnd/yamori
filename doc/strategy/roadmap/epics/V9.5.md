# V9.5: Volatility Surface and Yield Curve Management

**Labels:** `type/epic`, `type/feature`, `area/investing`, `area/platform`
**GitHub Issue:** TBD

V9.5 provides the shared-memory infrastructure for volatility surfaces and yield curves — the two market-data constructs that every other M9 epic consumes. By building and managing volatility surfaces (implied vol from market option prices, fitted parametric surfaces) and yield curves (bootstrapped from bonds/swaps/caps, interpolated for pricing), V9.5 stores these objects as shared-memory constructs (M1 Epic V1.3) accessible across processes. Changes to surface/curve inputs produce deterministic re-pricing through the dependency graph. Provenance captures the source data, fitting method, and interpolation scheme, making every surface and curve auditable, replayable, and bit-identical across platforms.

## Product Intent

Build and manage volatility surfaces and yield curves as shared-memory objects accessible across processes. Volatility surfaces: implied vol from market option prices (calibration against Black-Scholes-Merton), fitted parametric surfaces (SVI, SABR, local vol models). Yield curves: bootstrapped from bonds/swaps/caps (market quotes), interpolated for pricing (linear, cubic, Nelson-Siegel, flat-forward). Surfaces and curves are stored as shared-memory objects (M1 Epic V1.3) accessible across processes. Changes to surface/curve inputs produce deterministic re-pricing through the dependency graph. Provenance captures source data, fitting method, and interpolation scheme. Deterministic execution policy pins the fitting algorithm, interpolation scheme, and calibration tolerance. Output: volatility surfaces and yield curves with full provenance metadata.

## Users And Jobs

- [Quantitative analyst]: Construct volatility surfaces from market option prices (implied vol calibration) or fit parametric surfaces (SVI, SABR) through Yamori's dependency graph — receiving fitted surfaces with full provenance metadata.
- [Fixed income analyst]: Build and manage yield curves bootstrapped from market quotes (bonds, swaps, caps) with multiple interpolation schemes (linear, cubic, Nelson-Siegel, flat-forward) — receiving curves with deterministic re-pricing when inputs change.
- [Risk manager]: Compare pricing sensitivity across volatility surface specifications (implied vol vs. SVI vs. SABR) and yield curve interpolation methods (cubic vs. Nelson-Siegel) to understand model risk.
- [Tickoni tile]: Consume `getVolSurface(strike, maturity)` and `getYieldCurve(time)` as callable pivots that return interpolated values through shared memory, with deterministic re-pricing when underlying surface/curve inputs change.
