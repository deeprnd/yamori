# V4.19: Monte Carlo Summary

**Labels:** `type/epic`, `type/enhancement`, `area/finance`
**GitHub Issue:** TBD

V4.18 produces a distribution of intrinsic values from thousands of Monte Carlo runs. V4.19 converts that distribution into uncertainty summaries using GSL Statistics for distribution summaries and percentile calculations. It calculates mean, standard deviation, median, and percentiles (P5/P25/P50/P75/P95), and the displayed valuation range. It includes a reproducibility test that proves identical input, backend versions, seed, and sample count produce the same simulation output. The outcome is that the raw Monte Carlo distribution is transformed into an auditable uncertainty summary — not just a histogram, but a statistically grounded confidence interval with documented dispersion.

## Product Intent

Deliver the Monte Carlo summary engine: use GSL Statistics for distribution summaries and percentile calculations, calculate mean, standard deviation, median, percentiles (P5/P25/P50/P75/P95), and displayed valuation range. Include a reproducibility test that proves identical input, backend versions, seed, and sample count produce the same simulation output. The outcome is a statistically grounded uncertainty summary that transforms the raw Monte Carlo distribution into auditable confidence intervals with documented dispersion.
