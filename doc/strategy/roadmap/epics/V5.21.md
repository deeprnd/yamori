# V5.21: Peer-set Management and Company Catalog

**Labels:** `type/epic`, `type/feature`, `area/investing`, `area/operations`
**GitHub Issue:** TBD

Defines peer-set membership as a sourced input (persisted, not re-guessed per run). Manages the company catalog with canonical identifiers (ISO 10383 MIC, ISO 4217 currency, ISIN/CUSIP), Damodaran sector assignments, and geographic exposure records. Ensures sector assignments are consistent across valuations to prevent beta drift. The outcome is that every valuation operates on a well-defined peer set with known, persistent membership — the foundation for all comparative analysis.

## Product Intent

Deliver the peer-set management and company catalog engine: persist peer-set membership as sourced input, manage the company catalog with canonical identifiers (ISO 10383 MIC, ISO 4217 currency, ISIN/CUSIP), store Damodaran sector assignments and geographic exposure records, and ensure sector consistency across valuations. The outcome is a persistent, well-defined peer set that serves as the foundation for all M5 comparative analysis.

## Users And Jobs

- [Investment analyst]: Define and manage peer sets by company, sector, or geography — and reuse them across valuation runs without re-specifying membership.
- [Compliance reviewer]: Verify that every company in a peer set has canonical identifiers, sector assignments, and geographic exposure records on file.
- [Engineer]: Add new identifier types (e.g., ticker symbols, SEDOL) to the catalog schema without modifying existing peer-set logic.
- [Tickoni tile]: Consume `getPeerSet(name)` and `getCompany(id)` as callable pivots that return peer-set membership and company metadata.

## Success Metrics

- Peer-set persistence: peer-set membership is defined once and reused across valuation runs.
- Company catalog: every company has canonical identifiers (MIC, currency, ISIN/CUSIP).
- Sector assignments: Damodaran sector assignments are stored and consistent across valuations.
- Geographic exposure: geographic exposure records are maintained per company.
- Beta drift prevention: sector assignments are validated against prior valuations.

## Demo Moment

- Command or flow: `tickoni peer-set create technology --add STLA:MSFT:GOOG`
- Expected result: A persisted peer set "technology" with three companies, each with canonical identifiers and sector assignments.

### How Many Stories?

Four stories: peer-set persistence (S1), company catalog (S2), sector assignments (S3), and geographic exposure (S4). The peer-set is the foundation — everything else builds on it.

### Decomposition Rules

1. **Independent delivery**: Each story produces verifiable data layer.
2. **One capability per story**: S1 = persistence; S2 = identifiers; S3 = sectors; S4 = geography.
3. **Split by capability lifecycle**: S1 stores the set; S2 populates identifiers; S3 assigns sectors; S4 records geography.
4. **Closure story**: S4 returns complete company catalog with all metadata.

### Bad Splits (Avoid)

- "Company catalog" + "Sector assignments" as two stories. They are the same data model — sector assignment is a field on the company record.

### Story Line Format

Story IDs follow `VX.Y.SN`. Each story is independently compilable and testable.
