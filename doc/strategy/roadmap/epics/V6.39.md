Implements OBV, AD, ADOSC, and TA-Lib's candlestick pattern recognition API. Volume indicators produce cumulative or smoothed series from price + volume data; pattern recognition produces boolean or categorical signals from candlestick formations. Both are formula nodes on the dependency graph.

## Product Intent

Implement volume indicators (OBV, AD, ADOSC) and candlestick pattern recognition from TA-Lib as formula nodes on the dependency graph. Volume indicators produce cumulative or smoothed series from price + volume data, complementing the price-only indicators from V6.2–V6.4. Pattern recognition produces boolean or categorical signals from candlestick formations — the first V6 capability that translates discrete chart patterns into machine-readable signals.

## Users And Jobs

- [Investment analyst]: Compute volume indicators to confirm price trends and identify accumulation/distribution phases; use candlestick patterns for pattern-based signals.
- [Engineer]: Validate that volume indicators correctly integrate price + volume data, and that pattern recognition returns boolean/categorical signals for each candlestick type.
- [Compliance reviewer]: Verify that pattern recognition signals are deterministic and reproducible across platforms.
