# V1.2: Historical Metrics and ROIC

**Labels:** `type/epic`, `type/feature`, `area/investing`
**GitHub Issue:** TBD

Computes the historical evidence the valuation engine needs: NOPAT from EBIT × (1 − tax), derived invested capital from balance-sheet series, historical ROIC per period, net reinvestment, and reinvestment rate. Applies outlier filtering (negative and 3× median exclusion) to derive sustainable ROIC and reinvestment rate inputs for the growth model. This epic turns raw historical financial statements into the five-year evidence base that V1.3 (WACC) and V1.4 (growth model) depend on.

## Product Intent

Deliver the historical metrics engine: transform raw, period-by-period financial statements into the auditable, filtered evidence base that drives the valuation's growth assumptions. The outcome is that the engine knows a company's sustainable ROIC and reinvestment rate — the two inputs that determine whether growth is funded efficiently or destroys value.

## Users And Jobs

- [Investment analyst]: See the historical ROIC and reinvestment trend that drives the growth model, with outlier filtering documented.
- [Compliance reviewer]: Verify that historical medians used for sustainable inputs are computed via the documented exclusion rules (negative and 3× median).
- [Engineer]: Extend the metrics pipeline by adding new historical computations (e.g., working-capital intensity) that feed into the growth model.
- [Tickoni tile]: Consume `historicalMetrics()` as a callable capability that returns filtered, auditable historical evidence.

## Success Metrics

- NOPAT computed as `EBIT × (1 − tax rate)` for every historical period.
- Invested capital derived from balance-sheet series with goodwill included (D14).
- Historical ROIC per period: `NOPAT / InvestedCapital`.
- Net reinvestment: CapEx + ΔWorkingCapital − D&A.
- Reinvestment rate: `Reinvestment / NOPAT`.
- Outlier filtering: negative ROIC values excluded; values > 3× median excluded.
- Sustainable ROIC and reinvestment rate derived from filtered historical medians.
- Base-year distortion detection: current margin deviating > 30% from five-year median.

## Demo Moment

- Command or flow: `tickoni valuation run STLA --show-metrics`
- Expected result: A table of historical NOPAT, invested capital, ROIC, reinvestment rate, with outlier flags and the computed sustainable inputs.

## Scope

### In Scope

- NOPAT computation: `EBIT × (1 − tax rate)` using Arrow Compute compose.
- Invested capital: balance-sheet series composition (debt + equity − cash, per methodology).
- ROIC per period: `NOPAT / InvestedCapital` with null propagation.
- Net reinvestment: CapEx + ΔWorkingCapital − D&A.
- Reinvestment rate: `Reinvestment / NOPAT` with null-safe division.
- Fundamental growth: `ROIC × ReinvestmentRate`.
- Historical median calculation via GNU GSL statistics (median, order statistics).
- Outlier filtering: negative ROIC exclusion; 3× median exclusion rule.
- Base-year distortion detection: current margin > 30% deviation from five-year median.
- Base-year normalization: calculate normalized EBIT from revenue × historical median margin.
- Provenance: every computed metric carries its source period, formula, and input citations.

### Out Of Scope

- R&D capitalization adjustment (V1.3).
- Lease capitalization adjustment (V1.3).
- Stock-based compensation deduction (V1.3).
- WACC computation (V1.3).
- Three-stage growth model (V1.4).
- FCFF projection (V1.4).
- Financial statement ingestion (V2.11).
- XBRL mapping (V2.11).

### How Many Stories?

Eight stories: NOPAT (S1), invested capital (S2), ROIC + growth (S3), reinvestment (S4), median via GSL (S5), outlier filtering (S6), base-year normalization (S7), and provenance (S8). Each delivers an independently testable computation. Small scope — the epic is narrow (historical metrics only), so 8 stories is on the high side but justified by the number of distinct financial computations.

### Decomposition Rules

1. **Independent delivery**: Each story compiles and produces a verifiable computation.
2. **One capability per story**: S1 = NOPAT; S2 = invested capital; S3 = ROIC; S4 = reinvestment; S5 = median; S6 = filtering; S7 = normalization; S8 = provenance.
3. **Split by capability lifecycle**: S1–S4 compute the raw metrics; S5–S6 apply the statistical filters; S7 handles the normalization; S8 adds provenance.
4. **Closure story**: S8 ensures all computed metrics carry provenance — the evidence layer.

### Bad Splits (Avoid)

- "NOPAT" + "Invested Capital" as one story. They are independently computed from different balance-sheet and income-statement sources.
- "ROIC" + "Fundamental Growth" as two stories. They are computed together: `g = ROIC × reinvestmentRate`.

### Story Line Format

Story IDs follow `VX.Y.SN`. Each story is independently compilable and testable.
