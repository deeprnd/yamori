
# V9.3: Exotic Option Pricing

V9.3 extends Yamori beyond plain-vanilla equity options to the exotic derivatives that institutional traders price and hedge daily. By implementing barrier options (up/out, down/in, and variants), Asian options (average price, average strike), lookback options, and compound options through QuantLib's Monte Carlo and finite-difference engines, it delivers fair value, Greeks via finite-difference perturbation, and convergence diagnostics for instruments that require numerical methods rather than closed-form solutions. Deterministic execution policy pins the RNG seed, sample count, time step discretization, and variance reduction method, so simulation results are fully reproducible across all four target platforms.

## Product Intent

Deliver exotic option pricing through QuantLib's numerical engines: barrier options (up/out, down/in, and variants) via Monte Carlo and finite-difference, Asian options (average price, average strike) via Monte Carlo with antithetic variates, lookback options via tree-based and Monte Carlo methods, and compound options (call-on-call, put-on-put, etc.) via tree engines. These instruments require numerical methods rather than closed-form solutions, so deterministic execution policy pins the RNG seed, sample count, time step discretization, and variance reduction method (antithetic variates, control variates). Output includes fair value, Greeks via finite-difference perturbation, and convergence diagnostics — making every exotic price auditable, reproducible, and bit-identical across platforms.

## Users And Jobs

- [Options trader]: Price exotic options (barrier, Asian, lookback, compound) through Yamori's dependency graph — receiving fair value and Greeks with pinned simulation parameters that guarantee reproducibility.
- [Risk manager]: Compute exotic option Greeks via finite-difference perturbation for portfolio risk calculations — deterministic, auditable, reproducible with pinned RNG seed and sample count.
- [Quantitative analyst]: Validate convergence of Monte Carlo pricing by comparing results across sample counts (10K, 100K, 1M) and variance reduction methods (antithetic vs. control variates).
- [Tickoni tile]: Consume `priceExotic(type, parameters)` as a callable pivot that returns fair value, Greeks, and convergence diagnostics through shared memory.
