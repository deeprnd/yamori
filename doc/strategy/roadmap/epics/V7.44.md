
# V7.4: Risk Decomposition

**Labels:** `type/epic`, `type/enhancement`, `area/finance`
**GitHub Issue:** TBD

<!-- One paragraph: what complete feature this epic delivers and why now.-->

V7.3 built factor models from return data. V7.4 answers the portfolio manager's core question: "Where is my risk coming from?" Risk decomposition breaks portfolio risk into factor contributions, asset contributions, and marginal contributions. It computes component VaR, expected shortfall, and risk parity allocations. This is the analytics layer that turns raw factor model outputs into actionable risk intelligence — and it's what makes the factor model useful rather than just an academic exercise.

## Product Intent

Decompose portfolio risk into factor contributions, asset contributions, and marginal contributions using the factor model from V7.3. Compute component VaR, expected shortfall, and risk parity allocations. Use LAPACK eigenvalue decomposition for the factor covariance structure. Deterministic execution policy pins the decomposition algorithm, confidence level for VaR, and the risk measure (variance, VaR, CVaR, semivariance).

## Users And Jobs

- [Risk manager]: Compute component VaR and expected shortfall for regulatory and internal reporting.
