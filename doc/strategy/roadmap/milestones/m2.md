# M2: Grounded Valuation — Accounting Adjustments And Data Pipeline

## Description

Extends M1's valuation core with financial statement ingestion, XBRL mapping, R&D and lease capitalization adjustments, base-year normalization, and diluted share counting — the data integrity layer that ensures every input to the DCF is auditable and methodologically sound.

```text
raw statements -> XBRL mapping -> adjustments -> normalized financials
```

M2 closes the gap between raw financial filings and the clean, adjusted inputs that M1's engine requires. It handles the most common source of valuation error: inconsistent accounting treatment across companies and periods. R&D capitalization, lease capitalization, stock-based compensation deduction, and base-year margin normalization are all implemented as deterministic policy-driven transformations, not LLM judgments.

## Included Epics

### Epic V2.6 — Financial statement ingestion and XBRL mapping

Normalizes financial statements from XBRL filings into Yamori's typed internal representation, mapping US-GAAP and IFRS elements to canonical names. Handles the filing structure (income statement, balance sheet, cash flow, notes) and ensures every metric carries its source document, period, and currency.

### Epic V2.7 — R&D capitalization adjustment

Capitalizes R&D as an amortizing asset using sector-dependent amortization periods. Adjusts EBIT by adding back current-period R&D less amortization. Adjusts invested capital by adding the unamortized R&D asset. This is Damodaran's signature adjustment and was entirely absent in M1's raw valuation.

### Epic V2.8 — Lease capitalization adjustment

Capitalizes operating lease obligations using the methodology's supplied discount rate. Adjusts debt by adding capitalized lease obligations. Adjusts EBIT by adding lease expense less depreciation on the capitalized lease. Ensures lease treatment is consistent across EBIT, invested capital, and ROIC.

### Epic V2.9 — Stock-based compensation and diluted shares

Deducts SBC as a real operating expense rather than adding it back. Values outstanding employee options using the Black-Scholes framework (or treasury-method fallback when option-level data is unavailable). Adjusts share count for option dilution.

### Epic V2.10 — Base-year normalization and distortion detection

Applies the five-year historical median margin to detect base-year distortion (current margin deviating >30% from median). Normalizes EBIT when distortion is detected. Validates that stage-1 inputs derive from history, not assertion. Reports the normalization decision in provenance.

### Epic V2.11 — TTM calculation and source-priority resolution

Handles trailing-twelve-month (TTM) calculations for metrics reported on a fiscal-year basis. Resolves source priority when multiple filings or data sources provide the same metric (10-K > 10-Q > earnings release > analyst consensus). Records which source and vintage was used.

## Why This Is A Milestone

M2 transforms M1 from a pure arithmetic engine into a production-ready valuation system that handles the messy reality of financial filings. Without M2's adjustments, every valuation would be methodologically incorrect for companies with material R&D, leases, or SBC — which includes the majority of large-cap firms. M3 and M4 depend on M2's adjusted inputs to produce valid cross-checks and peer comparisons.

## Completion Signal

Financial statements from at least three companies with different accounting characteristics (high R&D, operating leases, significant SBC) can be ingested, adjusted, and normalized. The adjusted financials produce materially different valuation outputs than raw statements would, demonstrating the adjustments' impact. Every adjusted metric carries provenance tracing to the source filing and specific adjustment applied.

## Summary

A valuation is only as credible as its inputs. M2 is where Yamori stops treating financial statements as clean numbers and starts treating them as filings that require methodological interpretation. R&D becomes an asset, leases become debt, SBC becomes expense, and distorted base years get normalized. This is not cosmetic — these adjustments change every downstream metric and can swing intrinsic value by 30% or more. Tickoni's commitment to grounded inputs means no adjustment is optional.
